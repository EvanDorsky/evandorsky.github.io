As a critical technologist,
{:.ethics-lede}

I am interested in how the technology I build interacts with society. I know that [technologists already come to their practice with some values and ethics](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S8a1DascnZg), whether they know it or not. So I am developing and critiquing my own values and ethics, and I am bringing my fellow practitioners along with me - by hosting book clubs, teaching seminar courses, and speaking on panels.
